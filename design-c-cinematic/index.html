Young people cannot pursue opportunities they do not know exist. So we show them everything.

Some teens reach graduation unsure of their future. It isn't a lack of ability. It's a lack of chances to discover interests and experience possibilities firsthand. P.A.T.H. moves beyond career talks and lets students explore, ask, and connect with mentors for themselves.
