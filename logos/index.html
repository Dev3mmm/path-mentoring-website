P.A.T.H. logo concepts

Five directions. Each shown full size, then small on a dark and a bright background to test it.

1. Rising Road. A road winding to a sunrise horizon. Says "path" and "horizons" in one picture. Works as a round social avatar.

PATH●
PATH●

2. Trail Dots. Bold wordmark with a dotted trail leaving the last letter and ending in a destination dot. Loud and teen-friendly. The dot alone is a neat favicon.

3. Signpost. Three arrows pointing three ways: college, trades, business and more. Communicates the "many paths" idea at a glance. Trustworthy for sponsors and schools.

4. Steps. Four rising blocks in four colors, one per letter and one per stage (expose, explore, empower, act). Reads as growth. Modern and easy to animate.

5. App-icon P. A strong rounded monogram. Clean, institutional and sticker-ready. Safest option for schools, printed shirts and a favicon.
