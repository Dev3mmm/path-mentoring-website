Why we exist

You can't chase an opportunity you've never seen.

Many teens near graduation feel unsure about what comes next. It isn't a lack of ability. They simply haven't had the chance to try things, meet people and explore.

P.A.T.H. goes beyond talking about careers. We let students experience them, ask questions, meet professionals and discover for themselves.

Our belief

Exposure creates possibility. Exploration sparks curiosity. Meaningful experiences inspire purpose.

Mission

P.A.T.H. empowers young people to discover their interests, explore new opportunities, and develop a sense of purpose through exposure to careers, hobbies, education, entrepreneurship, and real-world experiences.

Vision

A future where every young person has the exposure, encouragement and opportunity to discover their passions and confidently pursue a meaningful path beyond high school.
