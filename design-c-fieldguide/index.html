Our purpose

Young people cannot pursue opportunities they do not know exist.

Many adolescents near graduation feel uncertain about the future, not from lack of ability, but from limited chances to discover their interests and experience different possibilities firsthand.

P.A.T.H. moves beyond simply describing options. We help students experience, explore, ask questions and connect with mentors, so they can discover possibilities for themselves.

Theory of change

Mission

P.A.T.H. inspires, exposes and empowers young people to explore the possibilities beyond high school through meaningful experiences, mentorship and career exploration, helping them discover their interests and build the confidence to create a purposeful future.

Vision

A future where every young person has the exposure, encouragement and opportunity to discover their passions and confidently pursue a meaningful path beyond high school.
